This is the 3rd iteration of Ripshout in like, man, almost 20 years.  Fuck when did I get old? Anyway. The first one was a winform, the second was WPF, the third one is still C# but it is my own framework, CascadeUI which I am incredibly proud of.  CascadeUI also makes use of my SharpImage and MediaKernel libraries that handle all image opteration and audio codes in pure C#, no system or C or C++, pure AOT friendly C#.  It has taken almost 2 years to get here but it was worth it to me and I hope that anyone bothering to read this rambling will agree.  The new version runs very light, opens almost instantly and does a slightly better job of getting new channels, finding cool looking backdrops and saving mp3 IDv3 tag information.  
Enjoy the music.
